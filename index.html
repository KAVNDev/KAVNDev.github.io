Debugging

Trace bugs across the frontend, backend, and database to their root cause.

For example, I once tracked down a student enrollment issue where records were appearing duplicated in the portal. I traced it through the frontend submission, found a missing transaction rollback in the backend service, and discovered the stored procedure was executing twice due to a retry loop. I added proper error handling, fixed the transaction scope, and prevented future duplicates.
